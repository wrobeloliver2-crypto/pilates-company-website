What we offer

Move, breathe, get stronger

A focused selection of what you'll find at our studio — from dynamic Reformer work to calm, body-aware movement.

Reformer Pilates

Our signature. A dynamic full-body workout on the Reformer machine that builds a strong core, better posture, flexibility and stability — in small groups, with lighter and more challenging variations for every level.

Mat & Equipment Pilates

Classic Pilates on the mat with small props — precise, mindful and effective. Perfect for body awareness, a strong centre and improved mobility, suitable for beginners and experienced movers alike.

Yoga

From grounding Hatha to gentle Yin — strength, balance and calm. A welcome counterbalance to everyday life that leaves you feeling open and recharged.

1:1 Personal Training

Private Reformer Coaching

Your personal Reformer session — more attention, more precision, fully tailored to you and available in English.

In English · 1:1

One coach, fully focused on you

Work one-on-one with your own personal Reformer coach. Whether you're brand new to the Reformer, want to refine your technique, or prefer a session built completely around your body and goals, you'll get calm, focused, personal guidance from the very first minute.

If you're recovering from an injury or training around a physical concern, your coaching can additionally be supported by a physiotherapist — so your sessions stay safe and tailored to what your body needs.
